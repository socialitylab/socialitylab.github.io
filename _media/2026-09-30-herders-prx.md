The team at PRX/GBH's The World reached out to Anne for an interview after reading [Anne and collaborator Bonface Osindi's science communication piece in The Conversation](https://www.socialitylab.org/media/2026-08-25-heat-herders) focused on how heat may be affecting herders' health in Kenya, the US, and around the globe.

[This interview with Anne and collaborator Eric Osoro](https://theworld.org/stories/2026/09/30/how-extreme-heat-takes-a-toll-on-herders-from-east-africa-to-the-us) aired on 200+ NPR stations across the United States on September 30, 2026, with an estimated audience reach of 2.5 million.
